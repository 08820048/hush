### 描述 · Description(2227/4000)

```
HushPass is a quiet password generator for the Mac menu bar. Press a shortcut in any app, and a strong password is on your clipboard a moment later. It is hidden from clipboard managers and cleared automatically, so it never lingers.

NOTHING LEAVES YOUR MAC
• No network access: the app has no permission to go online at all.
• No account, no analytics, no tracking.
• Passwords are never written to disk. The optional temporary history lives in memory and is wiped after 5 minutes, on lock, sleep or quit.

FIVE MODES
• Random: up to 64 characters, with or without symbols, look-alike characters optional.
• Passphrase: words from the EFF word list, easy to remember and type.
• PIN: avoids 123456, repeated digits and dates.
• Syllables: pronounceable groups that are easy to read out loud.
• Key: Hex, Base64, Base64URL or UUID for developers.

HONEST STRENGTH
Every password comes from the system's secure random source with uniform sampling. Entropy is computed exactly, including rules such as “every character type at least once”, so the strength meter never overstates.

FOLLOW A WEBSITE'S RULES
Type a website and HushPass follows its password requirements: maximum length, allowed symbols, repeat limits. Rules for 471 sites are built in and looked up on your Mac.

MADE FOR THE KEYBOARD
• ⌥⌘P opens the panel in any app without stealing focus; Return copies and closes.
• Space for a new one, arrow keys or the scroll wheel for length, 1–5 for modes, ⌘1–⌘9 for presets.
• Click any character to re-roll just that one, or drag the password into another app.

MENU BAR QUICK PANEL
Click the menu bar icon to generate and copy instantly. Right-click to see the password first, switch modes, change the length or pick a preset.

PRESETS
Save your favourite settings as presets and give each its own global shortcut that copies without opening a window. Export presets as a file to share one set of rules with your team.

AND MORE
• Large Type for reading a password into a TV or router, and a Wi-Fi QR code guests can scan to join.
• Shortcuts actions and a hush:// URL scheme for automation.
• Light and dark appearance, English and Simplified Chinese, VoiceOver support.

Privacy policy: https://hushpass.fyi/privacy/
```

### 描述 · Description(852/4000)

```
HushPass 是 Mac 菜单栏上的密码生成器。在任何 App 中按一下快捷键,一个强密码就已经在剪贴板里了。它对剪贴板工具隐身,到时自动清空,不会一直留在那里。

什么都不会离开你的 Mac
• 不联网:App 根本没有联网权限。
• 无需账号,没有统计,没有追踪。
• 密码从不写入磁盘。可选的临时历史只保存在内存中,5 分钟后、锁屏、睡眠或退出时销毁。

五种模式
• 随机:最长 64 位,可选是否包含符号、是否排除易混淆字符。
• 短语:来自 EFF 词库的单词组合,好记好输。
• PIN:避开 123456、重复数字、日期等弱模式。
• 可读:念得出来的音节组合,方便电话里告诉别人。
• 密钥:Hex、Base64、Base64URL 或 UUID,面向开发者。

如实的强度
每个密码都只来自系统的安全随机源,取值无偏。熵值精确计算,“每类字符至少一个”等规则都会如实扣除,强度条从不虚标。

按网站规则生成
输入网址,HushPass 会自动套用该网站的密码要求:长度上限、允许的符号、连续重复限制。内置 471 个网站的规则,在本机离线查询。

为键盘而设计
• ⌥⌘P 在任何 App 中唤起面板,不抢焦点;回车复制并关闭。
• 空格换一个,方向键或滚轮调长度,1–5 切换模式,⌘1–⌘9 切换预设。
• 点击密码中的任意一位单独重摇,或直接把密码拖进别的 App。

菜单栏快捷面板
单击菜单栏图标,直接生成并复制;右键先看密码,可切换模式、调长度、选预设,满意再复制。

预设
把常用设置存成预设,每个都能绑定全局快捷键,按下直接复制、不弹窗口。预设可导出为文件,团队共用一套规则。

还有
• 大字显示,方便对照着在电视或路由器上输入;Wi-Fi 二维码,客人扫一下就能连接。
• 快捷指令动作与 hush:// 链接,方便自动化。
• 浅色与深色外观,简体中文与英文,支持 VoiceOver。
```
